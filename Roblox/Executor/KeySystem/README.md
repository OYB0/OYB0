## 🛠️ Quick Setup
1. **Option A (Recommended):** Go to [oybofficial.com/keysystem](https://oybofficial.com/keysystem), paste your script, enter your PlatoBoost Service ID & Secret, and click **Build**.
2. **Option B (Manual):**
   - Place your protected script in `SecretCode.lua` and upload it to GitHub Raw.
   - Configure `KeySystemUi.lua` with your Service ID, Secret, and Raw script URL.
   - Distribute the loader to your players.
